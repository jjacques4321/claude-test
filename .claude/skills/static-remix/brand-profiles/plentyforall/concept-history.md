**Retrospective note (added after user feedback)**: this run fixed the
prior run's text/product collisions by defaulting most concepts to a
top/bottom or left/right hard-split template. That worked technically
(zero collisions) but made the batch visually repetitive despite fresh
copy — 6 of 10 concepts used a near-identical "clean zone above/beside a
centered or near-centered bottle" composition. **Going forward: solve
collisions with compositional variety (see SKILL.md Step 6), not by
converging on one safe template.** Frameworks with only one natural
product-placement option (iPhone NOTES has none; US VS THEM, Sticky
Notes, and Prop Testimonial have a structural reason to look similar
run-over-run since their whole mechanic depends on a specific layout) are
the exception — vary those by changing *what's* compared/propped/quoted,
not necessarily the layout itself.
